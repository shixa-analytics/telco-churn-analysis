# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

**Question:** Which customers leave, when, and how much revenue does it cost?

**Data:** 7,043 customers, 21 attributes (IBM sample dataset).
**Tools:** Python, pandas, matplotlib, Jupyter.

## Headline findings
- 26.5% of customers churned, taking $139K/month (30.5% of revenue).
- Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7%, versus 2.8% on two-year contracts.
- 47.4% of customers in their first year churn, falling to 9.5% after four years.
- Month-to-month + fiber optic + electronic check customers churn at 60.4%.
